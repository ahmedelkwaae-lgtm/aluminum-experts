About Us

Leading aluminium architectural systems specialists in Egypt and Africa — built on real experience, reliable quality, and more than 120 completed projects.

Our Story

We build beauty in aluminium
since 2012

ae metall was founded in 2012 as one of the leading companies in the Egyptian architectural aluminium market. We started locally and expanded to serve large-scale projects across Egypt and African markets.

We specialize in design, fabrication, and installation of aluminium and glass systems including facades, security systems, cladding, doors and windows, shower cabins, aluminium kitchens, pergolas, and all core architectural aluminium sectors.

Our team brings together engineers and technicians trained to the highest standards, using advanced measurement, manufacturing, and installation techniques to ensure an outstanding result in every project.
