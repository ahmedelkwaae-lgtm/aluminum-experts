Buying Guide

How to Choose the Right Aluminium System

A practical guide to selecting profiles, glazing, finishes, and hardware for your project.

← Back to articles
📅 January 2026✍️ ae metall team⏱ 10 min read

Start with purpose and environment

The right system depends on where it will be installed, the required level of security, the local climate, and the expected daily use. Coastal projects need strong corrosion protection, while hot climates benefit from Low-E glass and thermal breaks.

Compare the complete system, not only the price of the aluminium profile. Glass, hardware, coating, fabrication, and installation all affect long-term performance.

For coastal and humid locations

  • Use suitable anodized or marine-grade protective finishes
  • Select stainless or corrosion-resistant hardware
  • Verify the quality of gaskets and drainage details

For hot climates

  • Use Low-E glazing to reduce solar heat gain
  • Consider double glazing and thermally improved profiles
  • Choose finishes and colors appropriate for sun exposure

For security-focused projects

  • Use laminated or tempered safety glass
  • Specify multi-point locks and reinforced hardware
  • Confirm the installation details before fabrication

Understand profile classes

The profile is the backbone of every aluminium product. Thickness, geometry, reinforcement, and the quality of the extrusion influence strength, insulation, and service life.

Evaluate the installer

Even excellent materials can fail when measurements, fabrication, sealing, or installation are handled poorly. Ask for clear specifications, realistic timelines, warranty terms, and a documented handover process.

Conclusion

A reliable aluminium system is the result of a coordinated decision across profile, glass, finish, hardware, and installation. Our team can help you compare options and select a solution that fits your project and budget.
